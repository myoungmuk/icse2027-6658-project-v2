# Author’s note on the inquiry about IRB review

**Paper:** Beyond %Resolved:Reassessing SWE-bench Through Human and LLM Code Review (ICSE 2027, #6658)  
**Corresponding author who made the inquiry:** Geunseok Yang, Hankyong National University

In August 2026, Geunseok Yang asked the university’s Industry-Academic Cooperation Foundation whether institutional review board (IRB) review was needed for a procedure he described as “AI가 작업한 내용을 사람이 직접 검수” (people directly reviewing AI-generated work). People would assess AI-generated answers as correct, incorrect, or uncertain, and the researchers would report the percentage in each category.

The Foundation’s research support team replied by email. The Korean excerpt below is followed by an English translation.

> 본 연구는 IRB 심의 대상에 해당하지 않을 것으로 사료됩니다.
>
> “In our view, the study described does not appear to require IRB review.”

The inquiry did not specify the later study’s three graduate raters, its sample of 987 code patches, or its analysis of agreement among raters. The email records the research support team’s advice about the procedure described in that inquiry.
